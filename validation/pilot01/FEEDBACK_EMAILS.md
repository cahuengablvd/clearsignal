# Pilot feedback by email — Pilot 01

Созвонов нет — решение владельца. Всё письменно: пять вопросов, одно напоминание, ответ с
предложением пакета. Не уверен, что ответить, — вставь их письмо в чат с Claude.

## 1. Questions — 1–2 days after the report was delivered

Subject: `Five quick questions about the audit`

> Hi {{first_name}},
>
> Thanks for reading the report. Five quick questions, a line each is plenty:
>
> 1. Which recommendations would you actually use with the client, and which not? Why?
> 2. Are the buyer questions and competitors realistic for this market? Anything off?
> 3. Which parts would you show a client as they are?
> 4. What would you charge a client for a deliverable like this?
> 5. Would you run it for another client?
>
> Critical answers help me most.
>
> Alexander

Для GEO-агентств (cohort B) добавь шестой вопрос:

> 6. Compared with what you use today, where is this better and where is it worse?

## 2. Reminder — once, 4 days later, only if there is no answer

> Hi {{first_name}}, a gentle nudge on the five questions below. Even two or three answers help,
> and if it wasn't useful, that's exactly what I need to hear.
>
> Alexander

## 3. When they answer — thanks + pack offer

Цену называешь сам и спрашиваешь прямо — без «если вдруг интересно».

> Thank you, this is genuinely useful. {{one line reacting to something specific they said}}
>
> For pilot agencies there's a founding pack: three audits for {{pack_price}}, for any of your
> clients. Shall I send the invoice?
>
> One more thing: is there another agency owner you'd recommend for one of the remaining pilot
> spots? An intro or just a name helps.
>
> Alexander

## 4. After their reply

- «Да» на пакет → счёт из панели Stripe (Invoices → Create invoice).
- Заполни в таблице:
  - `pilot_status`;
  - `feedback_date`;
  - `client_price` — сколько они выставили бы клиенту;
  - `wants_another`;
  - `pack` — offered / yes / no / later;
  - `feedback_notes` — три строки: что понравилось; что отвергли и почему; одна дословная цитата.
- Цитировать их на сайте — только с письменного разрешения: *"May I quote your answer: with your
  name, anonymously, or not at all?"*
