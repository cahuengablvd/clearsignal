# Segment findings — who actually buys

Recorded from real conversations, not assumptions. Update as evidence arrives.

## Service businesses are not the buyer (3 data points, 2026-08-05/06)

The clinic owner, a personal contact given a full audit free, produced three objections over two
days. None was about analysis quality.

**1. "I never set up SEO, I pay Google Ads to be guaranteed top-3."** His mental model is: pay money,
get a guaranteed outcome, someone else does the work. That is a description of an agency retainer.
ClearSignal sells a diagnosis and explicitly refuses to guarantee placement.

**2. "Looks like a 15-page AI document. No human wrote it."** Produced `R20`. The report showed no
trace of the expert review that half the positioning rests on.

**3. "I can ask ChatGPT this for free and it makes the spec for developers."** He did exactly that,
got a well-designed Russian PDF, and preferred it.

### Why point 3 matters more than it looks

His ChatGPT document is genuinely good. It found cross-page problems ClearSignal missed (see `R22`).
It is free, prettier, and in his language.

What it does **not** contain: any measurement. Its headline "AI citation readiness: 46/100" has no
method behind it — ask again, get a different number. It never queried an engine about the clinic,
names no competitor, and reports no share of voice. It is a website review presented as an AI audit.

**He cannot tell the difference, and does not want to.** For this buyer, plausible structured advice
and measured evidence are the same product, and one is free. That is not a product failure — it is
the segment answering.

### Consequence

Stop pursuing service businesses directly. They remain reachable *through* agencies, exactly as
`CLAUDE.md` already ordered the ICP. The two-week test stays pointed at agencies.

## The open question for agencies

The same objection will arrive from agencies: *"why pay when ChatGPT does it free?"* The answer that
works for an agency does not work for a business owner:

- An agency cannot bill a client for advice the client could generate free in a chat window.
- An agency **can** bill for implementation justified by evidence. "You are named in 4 of 18 measured
  buyer questions, here are all 18" survives a client asking "how do you know?". "ChatGPT said so"
  does not.
- A number with a stated method is defensible in a client meeting. `46/100` from a chat is not.

**Whether agencies actually value that distinction is now the central question of the sales test.**
If they cannot tell measurement from opinion either, the product has a positioning problem no
feature fixes. Log every occurrence of this objection verbatim in `tracking.csv`.
