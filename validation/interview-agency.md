# Agency interview script — 25 minutes

Purpose: learn how the agency sells audits today and whether AI visibility is already a
client conversation — then make the real €149 offer. Ask about PAST behaviour, not
opinions. Never ask "would you buy this?" — ask what they DID.

Record (with permission) or take notes directly into `tracking.csv` fields. The verbatim
quotes matter more than your summary of them.

## Opening (2 min)

> Thanks for the time. I'm building an AI-visibility audit product and before I sell
> anything hard, I'm trying to understand how agencies like yours actually handle this
> today. 20-25 minutes, and I'll show you a real report at the end. Fair?

## Current process (8 min)

1. Walk me through the last audit you delivered to a client — what was in it, what did you
   charge, how long did it take to produce?
2. What tools does that audit run on today? What do you pay for them monthly?
3. When a client asks "why aren't we getting leads anymore" — what's your diagnostic
   process, step by step?
4. Has a client mentioned ChatGPT, AI Overviews, or "AI recommendations" in the last three
   months? What exactly did they say? *(If yes — this is the money question, dig: who asked,
   what triggered it, what did you tell them?)*
5. Have you tried checking what ChatGPT or Perplexity say about a client? How? What did you
   do with the result?

## The deliverable (6 min)

6. When you sell implementation work (schema, content, technical fixes) — what evidence
   convinces the client to approve the budget?
7. What was the last report a client actually READ, versus filed? What made the difference?
8. If a report names the client's competitors as "recommended by AI" — is that useful to
   you or dangerous? Why?
9. Do you deliver reports under your brand, or is the vendor's brand fine? Has vendor
   branding ever blocked a deal?

## Money and decision (4 min)

10. Who at the agency decides to add a new deliverable to the service list? What did the
    last such decision look like?
11. What's the last tool or service you bought at under €200 one-time? How long did the
    decision take?

## The offer (5 min) — this is the point of the call

Show the sample: **getclearsignal.io/sample** — walk the evidence page and the fix list,
not the cover.

> This is the audit. For agencies I run it as a pilot on one real client of yours: €149,
> human-reviewed, 2 business days, agency-ready PDF. If it lands with your client, you've
> got a new line item; if it doesn't, you've spent €149 finding out.
>
> Want to pick a client and run one?

Then STOP TALKING. The silence after the offer is where the truth comes out.

- **Yes** → take the client URL, competitors, ICP on the spot; send the checkout link while
  still on the call. `purchase_outcome: paid` only when Stripe says so.
- **Hesitation** → "What's missing for this to be a yes today?" Write the answer verbatim.
- **No** → "Genuinely useful. What would need to be true for an agency like yours to buy
  this?" Verbatim, thank them, done.

## Service-business variant (shorter, 15 min)

Same structure, swap sections 2-3:

1. How did your last three customers find you? How do you know?
2. Have you ever googled yourself or asked ChatGPT about your own service? What came up?
3. What do you spend on marketing/SEO today, and what does it visibly bring back?
4. When leads dropped last time — what did you do about it, who did you call?
5. What was the last €100-200 you spent on the business that felt clearly worth it?

Offer wording flips to first person: *"We test how ChatGPT and Perplexity answer real buyer
questions about your business, show who appears instead of you, and give you the fix list.
€149, reviewed by a person, 2 business days. Free preview first: I can run the score on
your site right now."* — running the free score DURING the call, live, is the single best
close for this segment.
